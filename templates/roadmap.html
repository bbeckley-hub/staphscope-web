🗺️ ESKAPE AMR Platform – Roadmap

✨ Our vision: To build an accessible, open‑source genomic surveillance platform for ESKAPE pathogens and beyond.

⚠️ A note on timelines: The features below depend on funding, collaboration, and community feedback. If you love an idea and want to see it happen sooner, reach out – your support (code, data, or resources) can move mountains.

🚀 In progress / near completion

  • Machine learning for phenotypic resistance prediction – Train models on AMR gene profiles to predict resistance/susceptibility to key antibiotics (e.g., ciprofloxacin, erythromycin, gentamicin). This would help labs without AST capabilities to infer resistance patterns from genomic data.
  • Phylogeny annotation tab – Upload a Newick tree; the platform will colour tips by MLST, AMR genes, and metadata, with PDF/SVG export.
  • Interactive dashboards – Replace static images with Plotly charts (gene co‑occurrence, resistance heatmaps).

📋 Planned (next 6–12 months)

  • RESTful API – Programmatic submission for power users and pipeline integration.
  • Batch comparison tool – Compare AMR/virulence/plasmid profiles across dozens of isolates side‑by‑side.
  • Automated PDF reports – Generate publication‑ready summary reports (including figures and tables).
  • User accounts (optional) – Save job history, share results, and receive alerts.

🔮 On the horizon (dream big)

  • Pan‑African phylogenetic surveillance network – Integrate with other African centres to share anonymised data and track high‑risk clones in real time.
  • Resistance prediction from whole‑genome data – Use machine learning to predict MICs and resistance phenotypes for all ESKAPE pathogens.
  • Mobile‑optimised results viewer – Designed for smartphones, the primary device for many African researchers.
  • Submission to public databases (NCBI/ENA) – With user consent, directly submit resistance gene findings to global repositories.
