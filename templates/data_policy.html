🔒 Privacy & Data Policy

Your trust is our top priority. This policy explains how the ESKAPE AMR Platform handles your data.

📂 Data collection and usage

  • We only collect the FASTA files you upload and the email address you optionally provide.
  • Uploaded genomes are processed only for the requested analysis (MLST, AMR, etc.).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for commercial or marketing purposes.
  • Job metadata (country of origin, timestamp, tool used) is stored anonymously for usage statistics and platform improvement.

⏱️ Automatic data deletion (cleanup)

  • All job directories (including input FASTA files, output reports, and logs) are automatically deleted 24 hours after job completion.
  • If you need to keep your results, please download the ZIP archive before the cleanup runs.
  • The cleanup script runs daily at 2 AM (GMT) and removes any job older than 24 hours.

🔐 Data sovereignty and hosting

  • Currently, the platform is hosted on a personal laptop in Ghana. We are actively working to migrate to dedicated server infrastructure.
  • Once hosted on local servers, your data will remain under Ghanaian jurisdiction, in compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
  • No data is transferred outside Ghana without your explicit consent.

📧 Email notifications

  • If you provide your email, we use it only to send you a one‑time link to your results. We do not store your email after the job completes, nor do we send newsletters or marketing emails.
  • Your email is never shared with third parties.

🍪 Cookies and tracking

  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • A session cookie may be used only for essential functionality (e.g., to remember your job ID). No personal data is stored in cookies.

🛡️ Third‑party services

  • The platform uses IPinfo to geolocate the approximate country of each submission (for usage statistics only). No IP address is permanently stored; only the country name is recorded.
  • Other external services (CDN for Bootstrap, Font Awesome) may log standard HTTP requests, but these are not under our control.

👤 Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your job data before the automatic cleanup (contact us).
  • Opt out of providing your email – no email, no notification.
  • Use the command‑line version if you prefer not to upload data to any server.
