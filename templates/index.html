Why the ESKAPE AMR Platform?

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a global health emergency, with low‑ and middle‑income countries bearing the highest burden. The ESKAPE pathogens – Enterococcus faecium, Staphylococcus aureus, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Acinetobacter baumannii, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, and Enterobacter species – are responsible for the majority of hospital‑acquired infections and are increasingly resistant to last‑line antibiotics.

Our platform provides a suite of free, open‑source, web‑based pipelines tailored to each pathogen. From a single FASTA file, you can obtain comprehensive typing, resistance gene profiles, virulence markers, and epidemiological insights in minutes – without command‑line expertise.

Developed at the University of Ghana Medical School, we aim to democratise access to advanced genomic surveillance and support the National AMR Action Plan.
